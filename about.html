Kara Rehman is a product designer based in Brooklyn, New York.

I design for people who want to build deeper relationships with the content and creators they love. Most recently, I worked at Vox Media where I focused on the moments where the editorial experience met the subscription product. My work distilled strategy into intuitive user journeys focused on conversion, retention, and loyalty for New York Magazine's subscribers. I also teach interaction design at Parsons School of Design.

When I'm not looking at a screen, I like creating things with my hands in the form of cooking, knitting, and sewing.
